# Source review for a fresh wrist AF evaluation

All paired participants in the AFPPGECG v6 release have been used for development or for the published independent analysis. Testing a revised alert system on a fresh cohort requires concurrent wrist PPG and ECG with enough clock information to place each decision relative to a verified AF onset and offset. Monitor exposure is sampled without using the rhythm label. Missing PPG and gaps remain on the decision clock so they count toward coverage.

| Source | Evidence for evaluation | Access and unresolved fields |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Pulsewatch | Wrist PPG and concurrent chest-patch ECG from 106 participants in two trials. The authors describe clock-drift correction and provide an alignment implementation. The ECG record is continuous and the wrist acquisition cycles on and off. | Provider approval is required. The released 30-second labels require an AF segment to be AF throughout, so they do not yield episode boundaries. Confirm whether the provider can supply ECG AF intervals, native clocks and PPG availability across the prescribed monitoring intervals. |
| Tampere PulseOn cohort | The study analyzed 30 participants with concurrent wrist PPG and Holter ECG. It reports 1,415 PPG hours and exact arrhythmia onset and offset review. | The authors accept requests for the sponsor-owned data. The paper describes stored beat intervals, rhythm status and ECG. Raw optical waveform availability remains unverified. A beat-interval-only release would support a separate evaluation. |
| Kuopio ambulatory study | The study used simultaneous 24-hour Empatica E4 wrist PPG and Holter ECG and reports episode results for 11 participants with paroxysmal AF. | The data availability statement directs researchers to the authors. A public native waveform fixture was not verified. |

Source details are described by the [Pulsewatch primary paper](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13123731/), [data project](https://www.synapse.org/Synapse:syn64402376), [clock alignment implementation](https://github.com/Cassey2016/PulsewatchAlignment) and [Tampere PulseOn study](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/cardiovascular-medicine/articles/10.3389/fcvm.2023.1100127/full).

For Pulsewatch, a small permitted fixture would establish the participant and session key, signal clocks, PPG gap flags and ECG interval format. The source uses 50 Hz wrist PPG and 250 Hz patch ECG. Its alignment procedure estimates clock drift across patch changes and maps PPG segments to ECG. The trial used patch AF detection with cardiologist review of suspected events. The fixture should verify that the native event annotations and clock mapping are available. Keep evaluated time and uncertainty on the original clock, including the five-minute wrist off periods. The metadata lists native signal folders and processed plots. Confirm the download scope with the provider. [Pulsewatch trial](https://cardio.jmir.org/2023/1/e45137) · [Access instructions](https://www.synapse.org/Synapse:syn23565056/wiki/608635) · [Kuopio study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8764282/)

The 30-second pipeline needs native waveforms and rates, participant and session linkage, an ECG–PPG clock map, reference-valid and unknown periods, ECG-adjudicated AF onset and offset, and the native PPG history preceding each evaluation block. Rhythm-blind monitoring time supplies the false-start denominator. A fixture can verify these fields and timing on a few spans before the model is frozen for independent scoring.

MIMIC PERform AF supplies paired ICU waveforms with whole-record rhythm labels. It supports the [separate reconstruction benchmark](../rddm/mimic-perform-waveform.md).

Two public datasets cover separate parts of the project. [PhysioNet Wrist PPG During Exercise](https://physionet.org/content/wrist/1.0.0/) supplies wrist motion and manually marked ECG R peaks for the [exercise stress test](exercise-stress-study.md). VitalDB supplies surgical plethysmography and ECG beat rhythm labels, with AF and flutter combined. The [VitalDB preflight](vitaldb-preflight.md) verifies the native clock for a labeled-window source study. A fresh wrist AF event validation still requires a source with wrist PPG and adjudicated episode boundaries.
