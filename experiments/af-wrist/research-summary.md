# Wrist AF development research summary

The project tests whether PPG or an ECG reconstruction can detect new AF episodes while limiting false starts. The original frozen template detected 155 of 260 complete episodes in its independent evaluation and produced 45 false starts in 125 ECG-observed wrist minutes. The evaluation and its error audit informed the new development experiments below. [Independent evaluation](template-independent-evaluation.md) · [Error audit](template-independent-error-audit.md)

On the original shared development clock, direct PPG detected 7/17 complete episodes with four false starts, template ECG detected 12/17 with six, and RDDM ECG detected 8/17 with twenty. All three use ten development participants and the same 46.5 ECG-observed wrist minutes. None reached 80% sensitivity on those held-out folds. [Decision clock results](clock-study.md)

| Experiment | Controlled comparison | Result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Pulse order | Train the same classifier with three chronological 10-second PPG bins or the identical bins sorted by feature values. Repeat with inner-selected C. | The chronological model matched 12/17 detections and increased false starts from 6 to 9. Inner regularization selection gave the same chronological result. [Sequence study](sequence-study.md) |
| Native PPG quality | Add native PPG quality and its products with two beat-irregularity measures. | The original split improved from 12/17 and 6 false starts to 14/17 and 4. Across five groupings, the candidate detected 1–14 of 17 episodes and reached 80% on one grouping. [Quality study](quality-study.md) · [Split sensitivity](quality-stability.md) |
| Onset-specific calibration and fitting | Penalize verified pre-onset false starts during cutoff calibration. Separately add an ECG-verified pair from a fourth participant to the existing 26-example onset fit. | Cutoff calibration and the additional pair left held-out detections and wrist false starts unchanged in their respective comparisons. [Calibration](onset-calibration.md) · [Native onset study](transition-native-study.md) |
| Stable training expansion | Add 107 disjoint paired windows in the corresponding person's training folds. Test two more AF windows from a seventh contributor separately. | The plain template gained detections in four of five groupings at equal or lower false starts. The expanded quality-interaction model detected 8–12 of 17 across groupings. The additional two windows left all reported AF outcomes unchanged. [Training expansion](training-expansion.md) |
| Lower-quality negatives | Replace non-AF training windows within the same participant and time bin, retaining all AF rows. | The plain template gained a detection at equal false starts in two groupings. The quality-interaction variant showed trade-offs or losses in all five. [Lower-quality data study](low-quality-training-study.md) |
| Shorter inputs | Compare trailing 10- versus 30-second windows at the same decision times in measured ECG and PPG-derived ECG. | Measured ECG improved from 3/17 to 9/17 with zero false starts in both arms. Plain PPG-derived ECG stayed at 12/17 and false starts increased from 6 to 9. The short PPG quality model lost six net detections. [Measured ECG](measured-ecg-context.md) · [PPG template](template-context.md) |
| Cross-cohort training | Train frozen ICU PPG or ECG-annotation models on their own participants and calibrate on wrist training participants. | The ICU score reached 14/17 with 9 false starts. Adding its score to the template gave 11/17 with 4 versus the original 12/17 with 6, within the 10-point sensitivity tolerance. Both were below 80%. The ECG-only source classifier fell to 4/17 on PPG-derived ECG. [ICU PPG transfer](mimic-transfer-study.md) · [ECG annotation transfer](afdb-transfer-study.md) |
| Raw PPG neural transfer | Train one compact CNN on the same 1,342 source PPG windows as the frozen ICU logistic model. Train only on source recordings and calibrate on wrist training participants. | Held-out wrist performance fell from 14/17 and 9 false starts with the source logistic model to 4/17 and 23 with the CNN. [Source CNN study](mimic-cnn-study.md) |
| Local waveform reconstruction | Fit a PPG-to-ECG template on paired wrist recordings inside every inner and outer fit. Apply the same AF classifier and alert protocol. | Changing only the documented archive delay raised external template QRS F1 from 0.211 to 0.640, close to the local template's 0.661. Waveform correlation stayed low. The fold-local AF pipeline detected 11/17 with 7 false starts versus 12/17 with 6. [Waveform study](waveform-study.md) · [AF study](local-template-study.md) |
| Synthetic sampling stress | Downsample each causal native PPG window to 50 Hz and back to 100 Hz. Hold each fitted classifier, cutoff and decision time fixed. | Both kept every detected episode and all 881 scoreable rows. Plain template false starts increased by one. Quality interactions retained the same four false starts. [Sampling stress](sampling-stress.md) |
| Additional complete episode | Retrieve a complete 5.756-second native event with 90 seconds of wrist history from a development participant with no original complete event. Replay each model from that person's original held-out fold. | The original and quality-aware alerts were active before onset. The quality model sustained a positive during AF, with no definite new start. The original benchmark denominators were retained. [Extra episode replay](extra-episode-study.md) |
| Dedicated onset classifier | Train early AF as a positive for a new-start objective and treat stable AF and stable non-AF as controls. Preserve the original nested groups and scoreable rows. | Detections and false starts changed from 14/17 and 4 to 7/17 and 7 on seed 2026, and from 1/17 and 0 to 0/17 and 5 on seed 2030. One participant contributed 13 of the 17 early AF examples. [Onset classifier](onset-head-study.md) |

Four development participants contribute the same 17 complete AF episodes. The largest contributes 13. In a separate attribution of the fixed quality model replays, the 107-window expansion gained 11 detections on the weakest split, with nine from the largest contributor and two from one other participant. On the original split it lost three net detections, all from the largest contributor. Both sets of results use the same people and need independent validation. [Participant attribution](training-expansion-participant-attribution.json)

The original-split quality-interaction comparison had a +11.76 percentage-point sensitivity difference and a −2.58 false starts per ECG-observed wrist hour difference. Paired participant resampling gave descriptive 95% intervals of 0 to +15.0 points and −8.0 to 0 starts per hour. Both touch zero. [Paired participant uncertainty](quality-uncertainty.md)

In a separate retrospective replay, the quality variants were fit and calibrated on the original fixed development participants, then replayed on the previously audited 25-person cohort. The historical template detected 155/260 episodes with 45 false starts. Quality alone detected 165/260 with 53. With interactions the result was 168/260 with 48. Both increased pooled sensitivity and false starts. All three fell below the 80% target. The largest contributor supplied 157 episodes. Average participant sensitivity fell from 55.7% to 50.7% and 51.2%. The paired intervals for changes in sensitivity and false-start rate span zero. [Retrospective quality replay](quality-retrospective-study.md) · [Retrospective uncertainty](quality-retrospective-uncertainty-study.md)

Retain the original template as the comparison reference. The quality models and stable training expansion are development candidates. A fresh cohort with aligned native wrist PPG, ECG-confirmed AF boundaries and representative monitoring time would test how they generalize.
