# Wrist AF development research summary

The project tests whether PPG or an ECG reconstruction can detect new AF episodes while limiting false starts. The original frozen template detected 155 of 260 complete episodes in its independent evaluation and produced 45 false starts in 125 ECG-observed wrist minutes. The evaluation and its error audit informed the new development experiments below. [Independent evaluation](template-independent-evaluation.md) · [Error audit](template-independent-error-audit.md)

On the original shared development clock, direct PPG detected 7/17 complete episodes with four false starts, template ECG detected 12/17 with six, and RDDM ECG detected 8/17 with twenty. All three use ten development participants and the same 46.5 ECG-observed wrist minutes. None reached 80% sensitivity on those held-out folds. [Decision clock results](clock-study.md)

| Pulse order | Train the same classifier with three chronological 10-second PPG bins or the identical bins sorted by feature values. Repeat with inner-selected C. | The chronological model matched 12/17 detections and increased false starts from 6 to 9. Inner regularization selection gave the same chronological result. [Sequence study](sequence-study.md) |
| Native PPG quality | Add native PPG quality and its products with two beat-irregularity measures. | The original split improved from 12/17 and 6 false starts to 14/17 and 4. Across five groupings, the candidate detected 1–14 of 17 episodes and reached 80% on one grouping. [Quality study](quality-study.md) · [Split sensitivity](quality-stability.md) |
| Onset-specific calibration and fitting | Penalize verified pre-onset false starts during cutoff calibration. Separately add an ECG-verified pair from a fourth participant to the existing 26-example onset fit. | Cutoff calibration and the additional pair left held-out detections and wrist false starts unchanged in their respective comparisons. [Calibration](onset-calibration.md) · [Native onset study](transition-native-study.md) |
| Stable training expansion | Add 107 disjoint paired windows in the corresponding person's training folds. Test two more AF windows from a seventh contributor separately. | The plain template gained detections in four of five groupings at equal or lower false starts. The expanded quality-interaction model detected 8–12 of 17 across groupings. The additional two windows left all reported AF outcomes unchanged. [Training expansion](training-expansion.md) |
| Lower-quality negatives | Replace non-AF training windows within the same participant and time bin, retaining all AF rows. | The plain template gained a detection at equal false starts in two groupings. The quality-interaction variant showed trade-offs or losses in all five. [Lower-quality data study](low-quality-training-study.md) |
| Shorter inputs | Compare trailing 10- versus 30-second windows at the same decision times in measured ECG and PPG-derived ECG. | Measured ECG improved from 3/17 to 9/17 with zero false starts in both arms. Plain PPG-derived ECG stayed at 12/17 and false starts increased from 6 to 9. The short PPG quality model lost six net detections. [Measured ECG](measured-ecg-context.md) · [PPG template](template-context.md) |
| Cross-cohort training | Train frozen ICU PPG or ECG-annotation models on their own participants and calibrate on wrist training participants. | The ICU score reached 14/17 with 9 false starts. Adding its score to the template gave 11/17 with 4 versus the original 12/17 with 6, within the 10-point sensitivity tolerance. Both were below 80%. The ECG-only source classifier fell to 4/17 on PPG-derived ECG. [ICU PPG transfer](mimic-transfer-study.md) · [ECG annotation transfer](afdb-transfer-study.md) |
| Raw PPG neural transfer | Train one compact CNN on the same 1,342 source PPG windows as the frozen ICU logistic model. Train only on source recordings and calibrate on wrist training participants. | Held-out wrist performance fell from 14/17 and 9 false starts with the source logistic model to 4/17 and 23 with the CNN. [Source CNN study](mimic-cnn-study.md) |
| Local waveform reconstruction | Fit a PPG-to-ECG template on paired wrist recordings inside every inner and outer fit. Apply the same AF classifier and alert protocol. | Changing only the documented archive delay raised external template QRS F1 from 0.211 to 0.640, close to the local template's 0.661. Waveform correlation stayed low. The fold-local AF pipeline detected 11/17 with 7 false starts versus 12/17 with 6. [Waveform study](waveform-study.md) · [AF study](local-template-study.md) |
| Synthetic sampling stress | Downsample each causal native PPG window to 50 Hz and back to 100 Hz. Hold each fitted classifier, cutoff and decision time fixed. | Both kept every detected episode and all 881 scoreable rows. Plain template false starts increased by one. Quality interactions retained the same four false starts. [Sampling stress](sampling-stress.md) |
| Additional complete episode | Retrieve a complete 5.756-second native event with 90 seconds of wrist history from a development participant with no original complete event. Replay each model from that person's original held-out fold. | The original and quality-aware alerts were active before onset. The quality model sustained a positive during AF, with no definite new start. The original benchmark denominators were retained. [Extra episode replay](extra-episode-study.md) |
| Dedicated onset classifier | Train early AF as a positive for a new-start objective and treat stable AF and stable non-AF as controls. Preserve the original nested groups and scoreable rows. | Detections and false starts changed from 14/17 and 4 to 7/17 and 7 on seed 2026, and from 1/17 and 0 to 0/17 and 5 on seed 2030. One participant contributed 13 of the 17 early AF examples. [Onset classifier](onset-head-study.md) |
| External exercise motion | Compare PPG and template-derived heart rate with manually marked ECG beats in 19 Shimmer records. Replay frozen wrist cutoffs and compare two motion-processing methods. | Direct PPG mean error was 25.62 bpm. Linear subtraction raised it to 38.13, and motion-coherence frequency weighting lowered it to 19.07. Among high-quality windows, large errors fell from 326 to 185 with frequency weighting. [Exercise study](exercise-stress-study.md) |
| Independent device motion check | Apply unchanged motion-coherence frequency weighting on Empatica E4 PPG and three-axis acceleration. Use two chest ECG channels to establish the heart-rate reference. | Across 1,809 paired windows from 14 people, mean error fell from 23.94 to 14.81 bpm. Running improved most, and some hand movements and walking worsened. The ECG consensus covered 1,813 of 3,026 scheduled windows. [ScientISST MOVE](scientisst-move-study.md) |
| Neural beat timing | Train PPG-only and PPG-plus-acceleration beat networks in five participant-held-out Empatica E4 folds. Freeze both for Shimmer with manual R-peak marks. | Compared with a fixed 300 ms delay, pooled beat F1 increased from 0.456 to 0.476 and 0.489 on MOVE. On Shimmer it increased from 0.340 to 0.422 and 0.394. Median absolute error on consecutively matched RR intervals was 47.5, 49.6, and 69.5 ms. [Beat CNN](beat-cnn-study.md) |
| Training-only pulse delay | Search 0–600 ms on each MOVE training group and freeze the all-source delay for Shimmer. | Pooled F1 increased from 0.456 to 0.464 in MOVE and from 0.340 to 0.345 on Shimmer. The frozen PPG CNN retained a 0.077 F1 advantage on Shimmer, with a higher median interval error among matched consecutive beats. [Calibrated comparison](beat-cnn-study.md#training-only-fixed-delay-comparison) |
| Neural intervals for AF | Keep the source beat CNN frozen. Refit the original wrist AF classifier with neural interval features in place of or alongside template features. | On the original split, detections and false starts were 3/17 and 2 after replacement and 9/17 and 5 after addition, versus 14/17 and 4 for the original quality model. [Frozen beat features](native-neural-beat-study.md#frozen-beat-features-and-af-alerts) |
| Native neural beat fine-tuning | Compare source-frozen, wrist-only, and source-initialized wrist training on the locked five folds and 223 verified native pairs. | Pooled beat F1 was 0.224, 0.457, and 0.456, respectively. The release-delay template reached 0.638. In the coarse-clock subset each CNN had higher F1, with support from 23 windows and two participants. [Native beat training](native-neural-beat-study.md#native-paired-wrist-beat-timing) |
| Direct raw PPG wrist CNN | Fit a 5,857-parameter raw classifier and the quality template control using the same 109 supplemental windows and nested participant folds. | The raw model detected 1/17 in both groupings with 3 and 4 false starts. The expanded control detected 11/17 and 12/17 with 1 and 2 false starts. [Wrist waveform classifier](raw-cnn-study.md) |
| Native wrist TCN waveform supervision | Fit waveform-only and QRS-weighted networks on the same held-out subject groups and compare with a template fitted on their training participants. | TCN waveform beat F1 was 0.070 and 0.075 versus 0.671 for the local template. The QRS timing head had F1 0.126. Both TCNs had waveform MAE close to a flat trace. [Wrist TCN comparison](native-tcn-study.md) |
| Native motion, waveform and AF | Align 832 native acceleration windows. Add motion coherence to the classifier or filter PPG and reconstruct template ECG. | The motion-feature model detected 8/17 with 3 false starts versus the quality baseline's 14/17 with 4. Filtering and retraining yielded 12/17 with 4. In 102 stable paired windows, filtering reduced QRS F1 from 0.666 to 0.648. [Native motion study](motion-af-study.md) |
| Ambient optical input | Match all 881 green PPG windows with native ambient light and add a coherence-weighted power feature. | Original held-out detections and false starts changed from 14/17 and 4 to 2/17 and 1. The weak grouping retained 1/17 and 0. [Ambient-light study](ambient-light-study.md) |
| Longer wrist false-start check | Select 30 five-minute blocks using wear times only and replay both original fold models with fixed cutoffs. | In 144 minutes 20 seconds of added ECG-observed time, the plain template had 27 false starts and the quality variant had 35. The paired rate difference was +3.33 per hour. No complete episode occurred in the added blocks. [Additional monitoring](monitor-extension-study.md) |
| Wrist hard negatives | Add 42 highly irregular non-AF windows or 42 random non-AF windows from the same people and time bins. | Original-group detections and false starts were 10/17 and 2 with irregular negatives and 9/17 and 1 with random negatives, versus 14/17 and 4 for the unchanged quality template. [Hard negatives](hard-negative-study.md) |
| Direct PPG spectrum with template | Add two PPG spectral features or all nine direct features to the same quality template classifier. | On the original split the two-feature model matched the plain template's 12/17 detections with 3 false starts versus 6. The quality template had 14/17 and 4. The alternate split stayed at one detection and zero false starts for all template variants. [Direct PPG fusion](direct-fusion-study.md) |
| Additional monitoring on the 25-person roster | Freeze four model fits and score 50 disjoint five-minute blocks from people excluded from training and previously evaluated. | Plain template gave 44 false starts, quality alone 76, quality interactions 71, and spectral fusion 71 in 241.5 ECG-observed minutes. No complete AF episode occurred in these newly sampled blocks. [Retrospective monitoring extension](retrospective-monitor-study.md) |
| False-start timing audit | Compare native and template-derived irregularity at every eligible false start and a nearby nonoverlapping negative decision. | No false start had documented AF in its 30-second input. Across 40–67 matched pairs per route, median paired ECG irregularity changes were close to zero while optical timing irregularity rose by 0.258–0.358. [Timing audit](false-start-audit.md) |
| Pulse peak agreement across filters | Verify each candidate peak in two filter bands and use only intervals with both original endpoints verified. | Relative to the broad-band pulse model, held-out AF detections declined from 12/17 to 9/17 with three false starts in both. The 77 stable AF pairs retained a median of 86.0% of intervals. [Pulse consensus](pulse-consensus-study.md) |
| Independent dual-wavelength PPG | Verify adjacent red pulses using measured infrared on the same 500 Hz surgical clock and compare with ECG intervals. | Across 61 paired windows from 21 people, mean paired absolute irregularity error fell by 0.056. Twelve windows improved, eleven worsened and 38 were unchanged. The same signal filtered twice had the same median error as red alone. [PeriWave dual wavelengths](periwave-study.md) |
| Optical placement and phase | Estimate an ear-to-finger PPG lag in an earlier input and apply it to later windows on the Auricle cohort. | Among 21 common windows, median interval retention rose from 58.1% to 84.6% with calibration. Median irregularity error was 0.082 for the calibrated route and 0.065 for finger alone. [Auricle dual placement](auricle-study.md) |
| Published multiscale optical peak detector | Run MSPTDfast v2 on original wrist PPG and replace only direct pulse interval features. | Median error on 77 stable AF pairs fell from 0.199 to 0.155. On the original alert split, complete detected events fell from 12/17 to 3/17, with three false starts in both arms. [MSPTDfast v2](msptd-study.md) |
| Timing sequence and distribution | Compare observed ordering of pulse intervals with the exact expectation for a permuted sequence. | The 2026 wrist result was 12/17 and 5 for observed order versus 9/17 and 7 for the distribution-only control and 14/17 and 4 for the original quality template. On the surgical source, distribution-only features raised mixed-negative AF-or-flutter versus ectopy AUC to 0.821 from 0.736. [Interval order](interval-order-study.md) |
| Paired surgical rhythm source | Join the VitalDB clock and rhythm annotations to candidate ECG and PPG channels. Check both waveform formats on six cases. | The paired catalog has 429 cases. Opening the original `.vital` files with the case origin raised agreement from 1,447 to 4,128 of 4,129 reference beats. The source groups AF and flutter under one label. [VitalDB preflight](vitaldb-preflight.md) |
| Paired surgical PPG transfer | Train on 30 native VitalDB cases. Replace 35 sinus negatives with the same participant's ectopy windows and test both models on identical source and wrist folds. | Source AF-or-flutter versus ectopy AUC increased from 0.620 to 0.736. Wrist source-only detections fell from 8/17 to 2/17, and false starts rose from 4 to 6. No source fusion matched template sensitivity within ten percentage points. [VitalDB transfer](vitaldb-transfer-study.md) |
| Alert rearming | Require two consecutive negative decisions to rearm a new alert. Compare fixed cutoffs and cutoffs selected under that policy using training groups only. | In the original partition, plain template changed from 12/17 and 6 false starts to 11/17 and 5. Quality interactions changed from 14/17 and 4 to 12/17 and 2, outside the sensitivity tolerance. Recalibration did not recover detections. [Negative rearming](negative-rearm-study.md) |
| Quality-conditioned confirmation | Require two consecutive positive model scores only if current optical quality is below 0.40. Preserve the original plain-template classifier and cutoff for retrospective replay. | The original development grouping retained 12/17 and reduced false starts from 6 to 5. The historical 25-person replay moved from 155/260 and 45 to 151/260 and 39. Additional monitoring moved from 44 to 41 false starts, with an interval spanning zero. [Quality-conditioned confirmation](quality-confirmation-study.md) |
| Long measured ECG benchmark | Verify 98 Holters from 93 people. Compare 10-second and 30-second measured input and their joint features on the same five participant folds. | Primary grouped detections were 443/767 and 289/767, with 4.04 and 2.00 false starts per ECG hour. The combined matched-calibration route had 249/767 and 0.92 starts per hour, 5.2 sensitivity points below the 30-second reference. [SHDB-AF reference](shdb-ecg-reference.md) |
| Matched early AF training with measured ECG | Replace each person's stable AF fitting examples with the first scoreable inputs within 20 seconds after verified onset. | The early-target model gave 427/767 and 5.61 false starts per hour, versus 458/767 and 4.08 for matched stable training. Median delay decreased from 13.0 to 11.4 seconds. [SHDB onset training](shdb-ecg-reference.md#train-on-the-first-available-post-onset-input) |
| Fold-local measured feature regression | Learn seven native ECG timing targets from wearable optical features, with all paired fit rows confined to the original fitting group. | Median irregularity error on stable AF increased from 0.198 to 0.390. Replacing or adding predictions reduced primary detections from 14/17 to 10/17 or 11/17, while false starts changed from four to one or two. [ECG feature regression](ecg-feature-regression-study.md) |
| Joint 10-second and 30-second wrist inputs | Concatenate both template feature sets while preserving the original fitting, calibration and replay support. | The primary joint route gave 10/17 and five false starts, versus 12/17 and six for the 30-second template. Its sensitivity drop exceeded the locked tolerance. [Joint wrist context](joint-context-study.md) |
| Independent validation design | Freeze the plain template and conditional confirmation before independent scoring. Plan AF episode precision and representative false-alert monitoring separately. | Illustrative 95% sensitivity precision of ±10 points at 80% implies 60 independent episodes or roughly 24–32 AF participants with three episodes each and ICC .10–.30. Burden sample estimates depend on the independent pilot variance. [Validation plan](prospective-validation-plan.md) |
| Wrist quality transfer across devices | Use aligned Galaxy Watch, E4 and Polar ECG windows with a fixed quality boundary and a simulated 25 Hz E4 control. | Across 618 common windows, the cutoff flagged 325 Galaxy Watch inputs and zero E4 inputs. It identified 297/569 large template timing errors on Galaxy and 0/533 on E4. [GalaxyPPG quality transfer](galaxy-quality-study.md) |
| Pulse strength and preceding interval | Supplement the quality template with optical prominence spread and the correlation of prominence with the preceding interval. | Primary detections fell from 14/17 to 10/17 as false starts fell from four to two. The alternate grouping changed from one detected episode to zero. [Pulse amplitude](pulse-amplitude-study.md) |

Four development participants contribute the same 17 complete AF episodes. The largest contributes 13. In a separate attribution of the fixed quality model replays, the 107-window expansion gained 11 detections on the weakest split, with nine from the largest contributor and two from one other participant. On the original split it lost three net detections, all from the largest contributor. Both sets of results use the same people and need independent validation. [Participant attribution](training-expansion-participant-attribution.json)

The original-split quality-interaction comparison had a +11.76 percentage-point sensitivity difference and a −2.58 false starts per ECG-observed wrist hour difference. Paired participant resampling gave descriptive 95% intervals of 0 to +15.0 points and −8.0 to 0 starts per hour. Both touch zero. [Paired participant uncertainty](quality-uncertainty.md)

In a separate retrospective replay, the quality variants were fit and calibrated on the original fixed development participants, then replayed on the previously audited 25-person cohort. The historical template detected 155/260 episodes with 45 false starts. Quality alone detected 165/260 with 53. With interactions the result was 168/260 with 48. Both increased pooled sensitivity and false starts. All three fell below the 80% target. The largest contributor supplied 157 episodes. Average participant sensitivity fell from 55.7% to 50.7% and 51.2%. The paired intervals for changes in sensitivity and false-start rate span zero. [Retrospective quality replay](quality-retrospective-study.md) · [Retrospective uncertainty](quality-retrospective-uncertainty-study.md)

Retain the original template as the comparison reference. The quality models and stable training expansion are development candidates. A fresh cohort with aligned native wrist PPG, ECG-confirmed AF boundaries and representative monitoring time would test how they generalize.
